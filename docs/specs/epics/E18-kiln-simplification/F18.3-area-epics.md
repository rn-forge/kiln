Replaces E1–E17 with one epic per area of the product, each describing kiln as
it is at v1. S18.3.1 and S18.3.2 come first, because they set how much each area
story writes and which areas exist. The area stories below are a proposal, and
S18.3.2 may merge, split or rename them. They are `New` and no commit names
them, so they can be renumbered.

Every area story keeps these properties:

1. The epic takes the next free ID after E18, uses the current spec format, and
   carries a `Replaces: E<n>, F<n>.<m>` line under its table.
1. Its features describe shipped behaviour. They are `Closed`, with `Iteration`
   Release 1, and their dates are the dates of the work they replace.
1. A story keeps its heading, its `**State:**` line, its `**Acceptance:**` list,
   one or two sentences on the outcome, and only those settled choices the
   code does not explain. It has no build list, no code or template text, and
   no review history. A code block is allowed only in an acceptance block that
   still runs.
1. Open work in the area (`New`, deferred, release-2) is carried into the epic
   with its state, `Iteration`, tags and entry criteria unchanged. `Removed`
   and superseded work is dropped, and `feature/v1` keeps it.
1. Explanation is at the level of principles and mechanisms. Lists of flags,
   task names and check IDs stay in the reference and the generated output.

## S18.3.1 — Where current behaviour lives

**State:** New

A decision story. `specs/_structure.md` is generated, and it sends "behaviour
that already exists" to `architecture/`. The owner's rule that explanations live
in the specs contradicts it. There are two options:

- **(a)** The template's rule changes for every adopter: area epics hold current
  behaviour and its reasoning, and `architecture/` holds only cross-cutting
  pages, or is retired.
- **(b)** Area epics hold the reasoning and the work, and `architecture/` holds
  the current behaviour, as `ci.md` and `tasks.md` already do.

**Acceptance:**

- The choice is recorded as a dated revision of the ADR that owns the docs
  structure.
- If (a): the generated `_structure.md` templates say so, `task kiln:apply` has
  re-rendered them, and `task validate` passes.
- The properties above are updated to match. Finding 8 of the idea is resolved
  or carried as an area story.

## S18.3.10 — The old epics are retired

**State:** New · **Depends on:** S18.3.3, S18.3.4, S18.3.5, S18.3.6, S18.3.7,
S18.3.8, S18.3.9

**Acceptance:**

- `docs/specs/epics/` holds E18 and the area epics only. Every directory from E1
  to E17 is deleted, and `feature/v1` holds them.
- E18's own links to old epics point at the area epics that replace them.
- E10 (deferred, with no area) is carried as its own epic or folded into an
  area, with its entry criteria unchanged.
- `specs/index.md` keeps its derived board. Its Order diagram and Upstream pins
  table are replaced by a short note on what is open. "Building a story" is
  cut to the steps that still apply to any builder, with no build-list or
  smaller-model wording.
- No row in the ideas index, and no idea page, links an old epic or feature, and
  the Source cells name no retired ID.
- `task docs:generate` and `task validate` pass, and the release pages' Scope
  tables are derived from the area epics' features.
