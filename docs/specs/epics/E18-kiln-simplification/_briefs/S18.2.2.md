# S18.2.2 — The ADRs, README, architecture, guides, reference and runbooks cite no feature or story

Brief for
[S18.2.2](../F18.2-ids-out-of-code-and-docs.md#s1822-the-adrs-readme-architecture-guides-reference-and-runbooks-cite-no-feature-or-story),
at `d006405`.

## Goal

No feature or story ID appears in `docs/adr`, `docs/architecture`,
`docs/guides`, `docs/reference`, `docs/runbooks` or `README.md`, so that
renumbering the specs (F18.3) leaves nothing dangling there. No ADR loses a
decision.

## Settled

From the feature page (owner, 2026-11-06), and the reviewer, 2026-11-17:

- **The rule:** nothing in those paths matches
  `\b[FS][0-9]+\.[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?\b` — in text, in link text, or in a link's
  path or anchor (`../specs/epics/E4-generator/F4.4-render-matrix.md`
  matches). Epic IDs (`E17`) are not part of this rule.
- **A reference that still helps becomes a sentence or a link to a stable
  page:** an ADR, or a page under `architecture/`, `reference/`, `guides/` or
  `runbooks/`, or a spec page whose path carries no feature or story ID
  (`../specs/index.md`, an epic's `index.md`). A link into a feature page
  goes, because F18.3 renumbers it. Where the reference only said which story
  built something ("shipped in S4.8.3"), drop it. Where it carried a reason,
  keep the reason as a sentence.
- **Other repositories' IDs count too.** `pykit S16.3.1`, `ngkit S17.2.1` and
  the links to pykit's and ngkit's `F16.3`/`F17.2` pages become words: "the
  repository's own cutover story", and a link to that repository's spec board
  (`https://github.com/rn-forge/pykit/blob/main/docs/specs/index.md`, likewise
  ngkit).
- **Dated revision notes keep the date:** `Revised 2026-11-09 (S16.7.3): …`
  becomes `Revised 2026-11-09: …`.
- **No ADR loses a decision.** In each ADR, the `## Decision`, `## Consequences`
  and `## Alternatives considered` sections keep every decision, consequence
  and rejected alternative; only the citation changes. A history entry such as
  "2026-11-13: amended by owner decision (S7.2.5): the developer surface gains
  …" keeps its date and what changed, without the ID.
- **Tests that assert on this text** change with it, in
  `tests/test_docs_runbooks.py`:
    - `test_runbook_has_no_init`: the needles `"S16.3.1"` and `"S17.2.1"` become
      `"own cutover story"`.
    - `test_runbook_pins_prettier_and_the_pages_record_the_revision`: for
      `docs/adr/ADR-0012.md` and `docs/reference/standard-repo.md` the needle
      becomes `"Revised 2026-11-09:"`; the two spec pages keep
      `"Revised 2026-11-09 (S16.7.3)"`. Split the loop into two. If another test
      fails on changed doc text, update its needle the same way and list it in
      `## Result`.
