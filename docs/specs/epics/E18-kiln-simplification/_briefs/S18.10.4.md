# S18.10.4 — A wildcard `==` is a floor

Brief for [S18.10.4](../F18.10-review-defects.md#s18104-a-wildcard-is-a-floor),
at `feature/v1` after `523016f`.

## Goal

A dependency on a workspace sibling written `==0.3.*` has the floor `0.3`, and
the check also reports a sibling whose current version is outside the whole
specifier.

## Settled

Owner decision: a sibling's current version must satisfy the whole specifier, in
addition to the floor rule.

- In `_problem` (`src/rn_forge/kiln/devops/requirements.py`), a `==` specifier
  whose version ends in `.*` counts as a floor: the floor operators become
  `(">=", ">", "~=")` plus `"=="` when `"*" in s.version`. Its floor is
  `Version(s.version.rstrip(".*"))`, so `==0.3.*` has the floor `0.3`.
- The exact-pin test stays first and is unchanged (`==` and `===` without `*`).
- After the floor test, `_problem` returns
  `f"{current} is not in {requirement.specifier}"` when
  `not requirement.specifier.contains(current, prereleases=True)`. Example:
  `a -> b: 0.4.0 is not in ==0.3.*`. For several specifiers the text is
  packaging's own order, for example `1.2.0 is not in <1,>=0.2`.
- The order is exact pin, no lower bound, floor above current, outside the
  specifier. A requirement with a bad floor keeps its current message.
- Rejected (do not build): checking the floor only, and a separate finding code.

## Current behaviour

- Probed with `_problem`: `a==0.3.*` at `0.3.2` and at `0.4.0` both return
  `"no lower bound"`, and `a>=0.3,<1` at `1.2` returns `None`.
- The new tests below therefore fail as noted in each.
