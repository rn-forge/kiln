# S18.2.3 — Release pages summarise scope and status

Brief for
[S18.2.3](../F18.2-ids-out-of-code-and-docs.md#s1823-release-pages-summarise-scope-and-status),
at `bb88a51`.

## Goal

Each release page under `docs/releases/` becomes a short summary of what the
release carries and where it stands, with no story ID. Explanation and history
stay in the specs and in git.

## Settled

Owner, 2026-11-17: **keep every section the releases structure prescribes, and
trim each.** The template (`docs/releases/_structure.md`, rendered by kiln) does
not change. Reviewer, 2026-11-17, for each section:

- **The rule:** nothing under `docs/releases/` matches
  `\bS[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\b`. Feature and epic IDs may stay.
- **Metadata table and opening paragraph:** keep the table as is; the paragraph
  is at most five lines on what the release carries.
- **Entry criteria:** a short list, one line each, no history.
- **Scope:** the derived region is untouched (`task docs:generate` owns it).
  release-0's `### Done before this release` table stays.
- **Decisions:** one line per ADR: link, date, what it decided. Drop the owner
  decision paragraphs; what they decided is in the ADRs and features.
- **Breaking changes:** keep the section where a page has it; do not add one.
- **Progress:** at most five lines on where the release stands now and what is
  next, as of 2026-11-17. For release-1, read the derived Scope table's
  states: every feature closed except F7.2 (the tagged release), F16.5 and the
  open E18 features. Drop every dated "As of …" paragraph.
- **Commits:** keep the sentence and the `git log … --grep` command; drop its
  output list and the "Earlier release-1 work" paragraph.
- **Exit criteria:** one line per criterion, naming the feature or epic whose
  acceptance block must pass. A criterion that names a story says what must
  hold instead (for example, "the cutover SOP, run on pykit and then ngkit,
  leaves no open absorb row"). Drop "Added …"/"Revised …" history.
- **Shipped:** keep the evidence: the commit, the CI run links and the table of
  blocks. Drop the narrative of what failed first and the story links; keep
  "Checked after the cut" as a list without story IDs.
- **Links to `plans/`** on a release page (release-2's intellibuild link) go, as
  plain text: `docs/plans/` is deleted by S18.2.4, which runs in parallel.
