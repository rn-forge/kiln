# S16.5.5 — The ngkit pin moves to v0.4.0

Brief for
[S16.5.5](../F16.5-rebuild-and-port.md#s1655-the-ngkit-pin-moves-to-v040), at

## Goal

A rendered Angular frontend adds `@rn-forge/ng` from ngkit's v0.4.0 release.

## Settled

- ngkit v0.4.0 is released with `rn-forge-ng-0.4.0.tgz`,
  `rn-forge-ng-bootstrap-0.4.0.tgz` and `rn-forge-ng-tailwind-0.4.0.tgz`
  (checked 2026-11-16 with `gh release view`).
- kiln still adds only `@rn-forge/ng`. The styling library is the application's
  choice and stays repo-owned.
- The `0.3.0` literals in `tests/devops/test_consumer.py` are fixture names for
  the consumer helper, not the pin. Leave them.

## Current behaviour

- `src/rn_forge/kiln/modules/node/angular.py:34-36`:
  `NGKIT_URL = "https://github.com/rn-forge/ngkit/releases/download/v0.3.0/rn-forge-ng-0.3.0.tgz"`.
- No test pins the version: `tests/modules/node/test_starter.py` imports
  `NGKIT_URL` (`:17`) and compares symbolically (`:56`, `:159`, `:180`,
  `:222`).

## Change

1. Test first (below), shown failing.
1. `NGKIT_URL` becomes
   `https://github.com/rn-forge/ngkit/releases/download/v0.4.0/rn-forge-ng-0.4.0.tgz`.
   Keep the parenthesised form and the docstring.

## Tests first

1. Append to `tests/modules/node/test_starter.py`:
   `test_s16_5_5_1_ngkit_pin_is_v0_4_0`, asserting
   `NGKIT_URL.endswith("/v0.4.0/rn-forge-ng-0.4.0.tgz")`. Fails before the
   change: `NGKIT_URL` ends in `/v0.3.0/rn-forge-ng-0.3.0.tgz`.
