`kiln init` is removed before the `v0.1.0` tag, and the cutover runbook becomes
the overlay procedure of [ADR-0019](../../../adr/ADR-0019.md): build a reference
repository with `kiln new`, bring its files onto a branch of the existing
repository, review one diff. The repository's own cutover story (pykit S16.3.1,
ngkit S17.2.1) lists each difference it keeps. Nothing about a repository's
nuances goes into kiln.

## Context

The 2026-11-09 reruns of the cutover on kiln `45a1e40` still left repo-owned
config unlike a new repository's. Closed stories S16.1.x, S16.3.1, S16.6.x and
S16.7.4 describe `init`; they keep their text as history and are not edited.

Read [the board's "Building a story"](../../index.md#building-a-story) first.
Three things hold for both stories:

- **Tests first.** Each test is named after its story ID (`test_s16_8_1_…`),
  written before the code, and shown failing.
- **Commands are `task` verbs.** `task test` runs the whole suite (it passes no
  arguments to pytest). Never run `uv`, `pytest`, `ruff` or `kiln` directly.
- **Do not commit.** The owner reviews the diff.
