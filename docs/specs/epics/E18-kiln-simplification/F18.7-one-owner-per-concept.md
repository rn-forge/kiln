Several facts are re-derived wherever they are needed, and the copies have
started to disagree. Defect 8 is one result: the Nx project name is `web` in
cicd and `Path(web_dir).name` in tasks. This feature gives each fact a single
owner, and has every caller read it from there. Fixing the disagreements
themselves is [F18.10](F18.10-review-defects.md)'s work. Here each copy keeps
its current answer.

Every story keeps these properties:

1. No rendered file changes. `task kiln:apply` reports nothing, and the goldens
   are unchanged.
1. The old helper or literal is deleted, not left beside the new owner.

Line numbers are at `6b3549b`.
